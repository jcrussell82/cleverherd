Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

How big is the community?

Small — we’re just getting started. We’re building a founding community of professionals who want to work together, and our first initiative is to create ten paying projects as a group. We won’t pretend to be bigger than we are.

Is Cleverherd free for professionals?

Yes. We won't charge people just to look for work. In future Cleverherd may earn a fee on completed client projects that businesses bring to the platform — we’ll be upfront about any fee before it applies.

Will I be paid for joining an initiative?

Not automatically. Joining an initiative or workstream doesn’t guarantee compensation, commissions or ownership. When a paying project comes in, the people doing the work agree payment terms with the client in writing before work begins.

How does equity work on a team?

Each team agrees on roles, commitments and ownership before work starts, documented in written agreements that cover vesting, departures and intellectual property. Cleverherd records contributions transparently, but it never turns hours into a percentage automatically.

Do I need my own idea to join?

Not at all. Most members start by finding paid work or joining someone else's team. Labs is there whenever you want to share an idea — or join one worth pursuing.

Can I invest in startups today?

No. Early projects can show non-binding interest from supporters — no money is collected. Equity investment would only ever be offered through an appropriately registered partner and in line with securities laws.

How do businesses hire a team?

Submit a project with your goals, budget and timeline. A person on our team reviews every inquiry, gets back to you, and — if it’s a fit — helps assemble members with the right mix of skills. Scope, price and payment terms are agreed directly in writing before work begins.

What if a team member stops contributing?

That's exactly what upfront agreements and contribution records are for. Vesting and departure terms are set before work begins, so everyone knows what happens if commitments change.

Home/The First 10 Opportunities

The First 10 Opportunities.

Let’s create our first 10 paying projects together. Our first community initiative brings together people with complementary skills to generate real business for the Cleverherd community.

The mission

Most platforms start by promising a marketplace full of clients. We’re starting honestly: we have none yet. Instead of waiting for work to appear, members pool their skills to find it — through business development, marketing, research, networking and outreach — and then deliver it together.

The goal is ten paying projects. We’re starting from zero, and we’ll only count work that is confirmed and paid.

How it works

  • Create a free account and set up your profile.
  • Join the initiative and choose one or more workstreams.
  • Work in the open: each workstream has a shared task board, milestones, a leads pipeline and a discussion.
  • Turn leads into projects: when a business wants help, the work is posted as an opportunity (reviewed before it goes live) and members apply alone or as a team.
  • Agree terms, then deliver: scope, price and payment are agreed in writing with the client before work starts.

Workstreams

  • Business development — identify prospective customers, develop relationships and pursue opportunities.
  • Marketing — help promote Cleverherd, develop campaigns, create content and attract businesses.
  • Research — identify underserved markets, evaluate customer needs and discover potential opportunities.
  • Design and development — create materials, improve the platform and help deliver projects.
  • Operations and project management — organize teams, coordinate work, establish processes and help ensure successful delivery.

Please read before joining

Joining the initiative or a workstream does not guarantee compensation, commissions or ownership. Contributions are recorded so effort stays visible, but they don’t automatically turn into pay or equity. Any paid or ownership-related work must be agreed separately, in writing, by the people involved before that work begins.

Home/Community guidelines

Community guidelines.

Cleverherd works when people can trust each other. These guidelines apply everywhere on the platform.

Draft — pending review. Final wording to be reviewed alongside the Terms.

Be honest

  • Describe your skills, experience and availability accurately.
  • State compensation precisely. Never present commission, revenue share or equity as guaranteed pay.
  • Don’t exaggerate traction, customers, revenue or team size for ideas and ventures.

Agree before you work

  • Put scope, payment, ownership and IP in writing before paid or ownership-related work starts.
  • Honor what you agreed, and say early if your availability changes.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, hate, threats or discrimination.
  • Disagree with ideas, not people.

Not allowed

  • Spam, bulk or automated messaging, and buying or scraping contact lists.
  • Asking anyone to pay to apply for work, buy “starter kits” or recruit others for pay (pyramid schemes).
  • Offering or soliciting investments, securities or loans.
  • Collecting other members’ personal data for purposes they didn’t agree to.
  • Impersonation, fake accounts or misleading listings.

Reporting

Use the Report link on any profile, listing, idea, comment, team or conversation. Reports go to Cleverherd admins and are kept confidential. Admins may hide content, reject listings or suspend accounts. If you’re in danger, contact local emergency services first.
